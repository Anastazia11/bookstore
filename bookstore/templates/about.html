About

Bookstore is a small online store where you can browse books, read ratings, and choose to buy or rent. It was built as a Software Design course project.

How it works: the web pages are written with Python (Flask), and all books, authors, publishers, ratings and carts are stored in a MySQL database.

What you can do: search by title, author, genre or publisher; open a book to see its details and similar titles; like or dislike it; rate it from 1 to 5; and add it to your cart.
